## Plots for the pre-pilot and pilot studies in 2025. 
For each study, there is a plot for:
1. Histogram of how long it took users to complete the study (duration).
2. Mean and standard error of the mean for Alex and AP-5 for each ACxRA combination, and for the user's confidence in their response.
3. Mean and standard error of the mean for Alex and AP-5 for each treatment of bias (bias against agent 0, bias against agent 1, bias against none, bias against both agents), and for the user's confidence in their response.
4. Mean and standard error of the mean for Alex and AP-5 for each treatment of information (full/partial), and for the user's confidence in their response.
5. Mean and standard error of the mean for Alex and AP-5 for each treatment of number of counterfactuals shown (0 for factual-only, 1-CF-Agent-0 indicates 1 CF shown for Agent 0, 1-CF-Agent-1 indicates 1 CF shown for Agent 1, 2-CF-Agent-both indicates both CFs shown), and for the user's confidence in their response.
